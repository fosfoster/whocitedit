2003 · Journal of Statistical Software · 1,530 citations · 0 from inside this corpus

John Fox

This paper describes the implementation in R of a method for tabular or graphical display of terms in a complex generalised linear model. By complex, I mean a model that contains terms related by marginality or hierarchy, such as polynomial terms, or main effects and interactions. I call these tables or graphs effect displays.
